ABOUT DR. YOGITHA

A specialist who sees the whole picture.

Rheumatology • Immunology • Autoimmune Diseases

I am a consultant physician and well-trained rheumatologist based in Vijayawada, with 5 years of experience diagnosing and treating musculoskeletal disorders, allergies, reproductive immunology, and systemic autoimmune conditions.

My mission is to help you regain optimal joint and muscle function. Using advanced diagnostic techniques, whether you are dealing with arthritis, lupus, or any other rheumatic condition, I am here to navigate your journey to better health with compassion and expertise.

“My mission is to help you regain and maintain optimal joint function, ensuring you live a life of comfort and mobility.” — Dr. Yogitha
